About

Building systems end to end, from raw data to the application on top of it.

I am a Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI) in Skopje, working toward a 240 ECTS degree with an expected graduation in May 2027.

I learn by building systems end to end: moving data from operational sources into reliable pipelines, analytical models, and applications people can use.

My strongest interests are in data engineering, AI and machine learning, and backend software engineering. I care most about validation, repeatable jobs, and data models that preserve history as source systems change.

I am currently contributing to the VEZILKA research project at FINKI, working on LLM training tooling and fine-tuning experiments for Macedonian-language NLP.

Since October 2026 I have also been a research intern at the Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts (MANU), studying the mechanistic interpretability of large language models. My research investigates reinforcement learning and mode collapse in risky-choice prediction, including whether an associated internal direction can be identified and causally ablated.
