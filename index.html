About

Building systems end to end, from raw data to the application on top of it.

I am a Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI) in Skopje, working toward a 240 ECTS degree with an expected graduation in May 2027.

Most of what I know I have learned by building things large enough to break. That has pulled me across several areas that turn out to be one continuous problem: getting data out of operational systems, processing it reliably, modelling it so it answers real questions, training models on top of it, and wrapping the whole thing in an application somebody can use.

My strongest interests are in data engineering, AI and machine learning, and backend software engineering. I care most about the parts that decide whether a system survives contact with reality: what happens to bad records, whether a job can safely be rerun, whether history stays correct after the source system changes, and whether the database enforces its own constraints.

I am currently contributing to the VEZILKA research project at FINKI, working on LLM training tooling and fine-tuning experiments for Macedonian-language NLP.
